1. Executive Product Definition
VAJRA is a proposed multimodal convective intelligence platform for SIH26084. It transforms atmospheric observations into structured convective-cell state, tracking, short-horizon evolution forecasts, evidence-gated hazard intelligence, uncertainty and GIS decision support.
VAJRA is NOT positioned as “another AI weather app” or as a replacement for established meteorological nowcasting. The project research shows that India already has substantial radar/satellite/lightning nowcasting infrastructure; therefore the defensible product thesis is the translation layer from evolving forecast fields to specific operationally relevant locations and corridors. fileciteturn6file0
1.1 Product North Star
OBSERVE → ALIGN → DETECT → TRACK → UNDERSTAND → FORECAST → QUANTIFY UNCERTAINTY → LOCALIZE → VISUALIZE → VERIFY
Core question: “What is the predicted convective hazard around this selected location or route during the next several hours, and when is the evolving storm expected to intersect it?”
1.2 ★ Flagship USP: LOCATION-FIRST CONVECTIVE INTELLIGENCE
The product should make location intelligence a first-class engine, not a map decoration.
Example workflow: an authorized user selects Jaydev Vihar Square, Bhubaneswar. VAJRA evaluates the selected coordinate against the current convective-cell state, predicted trajectory/field and evidence-supported hazards. The UI then presents the evolving hazard timeline and ETA only when a valid spatial/temporal intersection exists.
A second workflow accepts a corridor such as Jaydev Vihar → Master Canteen → Kalinga Nagar → Tamando. VAJRA evaluates where and when the forecast hazard field intersects the corridor. This is a proposed product capability, not a claim about an existing operational government service.

5.4 ETA rules
ETA is a computed scientific/product output, not a decorative timer. It must be derived from a forecast trajectory or evolving hazard field and a defined spatial intersection. If track continuity, time alignment, geometry or uncertainty prevents a defensible estimate, return UNKNOWN/NOT COMPUTABLE.
Do not calculate ETA by blindly dividing straight-line distance by current speed. Store the exact ETA method in provenance.
5.5 Location timeline
Provide T+00 through T+360 or the configured horizon, with every displayed point tied to a backend forecast record. After replay reveal, show observed state alongside forecast state.
5.6 Route intelligence boundary
The MVP should report forecast intersection/exposure, not autonomously tell a person to travel or not travel. Any operational recommendation must be based on a separately defined and validated rule.

Show data freshness.
Show available/missing modalities.
